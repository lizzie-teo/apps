2. Agreeing to these terms

You need to be 18 or older, and able to make your own decisions about your health care, to write an advance care directive.

3. What the website is, and isn't

The website asks you questions about your values and your wishes for future health care. It turns your answers into an advance care directive that you can sign, have witnessed, download as a PDF and share.

Not legal or medical advice

The website follows the NSW Health advance care planning framework, but it doesn't give legal or medical advice. It can't tell you whether a choice is right for you. Talk to your doctor about your health and your treatment choices. Talk to a lawyer if you have questions about the law.

New South Wales only

The website is made for New South Wales. Other states and territories, and other countries, have different laws and forms. If you live outside NSW, check what applies where you live.

Your directive is up to you

You decide what goes into your directive, and you're responsible for checking that it says what you want. Health professionals must follow a valid directive, but in some situations the law lets them act differently. We can't promise how a directive will be used.

Keep it up to date

Read your directive again when your health or your wishes change. Give the newest copy to your doctor and to the people close to you.

4. Your private link

  • You don't need an account. The website saves your answers against a random code in your link.
  • Anyone who has your link can see your directive and your signatures. Only share it with people you trust, such as your family, your substitute decision-maker or your doctor.
  • We don't know your name or email address, so we can't send you a lost link. Keep it somewhere safe, and download the PDF.

5. What you write

It's yours

Your answers, your values, your notes and your signatures all belong to you.

To run the website, we need your permission to store, copy and back up these things, and show them to anyone who opens your link. You give us that permission, for that purpose only. We never sell what you write or use it for advertising.

Nobody is watching

Nobody reviews your directive, and nobody is told about it. Your doctor, your family and your hospital don't get a copy unless you give them one.

Deleting

To delete your directive, email us your link and ask us to delete it. Deleting can't be undone, so download the PDF first if you want to keep a copy.

6. Signing and witnessing

  • You can sign your directive on screen. Your signature is saved as a picture with your name and the time you signed.
  • You can share your link with a witness, who signs on their own device. Their name and signature are saved with your directive.
  • A witness isn't required in NSW, but NSW Health strongly recommends one. Choose an adult who isn't your substitute decision-maker and won't benefit from your decisions.
  • Only sign as yourself. A witness must only sign if they saw you sign, or if you told them the signature is yours.

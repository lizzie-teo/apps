Advance Care Directive

A free website that helps you write down your wishes for future health care in New South Wales. This page has help, contact details and the legal documents.

This website isn't for emergencies

If you or someone else needs urgent medical help, call 000.

This website isn't legal or medical advice. For advice about your directive, talk to your doctor or a lawyer.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your answers are saved to a private link. Keep that link somewhere safe. Anyone who has it can see your directive.

I've lost my link

We can't find your directive without the link, because we don't know your name or email address. If you downloaded the PDF, use that copy. If not, you can start a new directive.

Is my directive legal?

Advance care directives are legally recognised in NSW. A witness isn't required, but NSW Health strongly recommends one. Give a copy to your doctor and to the people close to you.

How do I delete my directive?

Email us your link and ask us to delete it. We'll delete your answers and signatures and tell you when it's done.

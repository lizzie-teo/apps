1. Introduction

This document sets out the privacy policy of [insert] (referred to in this privacy policy as ‘we’, ‘us’, or ‘our’).

We take our privacy obligations seriously and we’ve created this privacy policy to explain how we store, maintain, use and disclose personal information.

We are committed to preventing serious invasions of privacy and ensuring the protection of your personal information, so you can contact us using the details below if you have any questions or concerns.

By providing personal information to us, you consent to our storage, maintenance, use and disclosure of personal information in accordance with this privacy policy, except where express consent is required (such as for sensitive information). We may change this privacy policy from time to time by posting an updated copy on our website. Where practicable, we will notify you of material changes by displaying a prominent notice on our website. 2. Types of personal information we collect

The personal information we collect may include the following (and may include sensitive information such as health information relating to your care preferences, including advance care directive choices about dementia care and end-of-life treatment):

(a)name;

(b)answers to care preference questions and free-text notes;

(c)electronic signature images (drawn on-screen by the person and their witness);

(d)chosen value words (e.g. “Dignity”, “Family”) and optional values notes;

(e)full name of the person creating the directive and their witness;

(f)the unique directive identifier and link associated with your directive;

(g)any other information you submit through the app in connection with your advance care directive;

(h)your device identity and type, I.P. address, and standard web log information.

(i)information about your health, medical preferences, and personal circumstances relevant to your advance care directive;

(j)information provided by your witness in connection with witnessing your directive;

(k)your device identity and type, I.P. address, and standard web log information;

(l)information about your witness, including their full name and electronic signature, as provided during the witnessing process; and

(m)any other information you voluntarily provide to us through our website in connection with your advance care directive.

3. [Optional] Personal information of minors

If we collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18, we will assess their capacity to consent on a case-by-case basis in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APP) Guidelines. As a general principle, an individual under the age of 18 may have the capacity to consent if they demonstrate sufficient maturity to understand the nature and consequences of what is being proposed. Where it is determined that the individual does not have such capacity, we will seek verifiable consent from a parent or guardian.

If it is not practicable to assess capacity on a case-by-case basis, we will generally assume that individuals aged 15 and above have the capacity to provide consent, unless there are reasonable grounds to believe otherwise. Parents or guardians may contact us at usefulthings.studio@gmail.com to review, manage, or request deletion of their child’s personal information.

4. How we collect personal information

We may collect personal information either directly from you or from third parties, including where you:

(a)contact us through our website;

(b)use our website to create an advance care directive;

(c)complete the advance care directive process on our website, including answering care preference questions, providing your name, and signing;

(d)communicate with us via email or through our website;

(e)interact with our website; and

(f)complete the advance care directive creation and witnessing process on our website.

We may also collect personal information from you when you use or access our website. This may be done through use of ‘cookies’ or other similar technologies. Cookies are small files that store information on your computer, mobile phone or other device. We do not currently use third-party analytics or tracking tools. If you do not wish information to be stored as a cookie, you can disable cookies in your web browser.

1. Who we are

The Advance Care Directive website is made and run by Lizzie Teo, a sole trader in Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Lizzie. We handle your information under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

This policy goes with our terms and conditions. You can reach us at [contact email].

2. What we collect

Your directive

When you start, the website makes a random code for your link. Everything you save is linked to that code, not to your name or email address. We keep:

  • your answers to the questions, and any notes you write
  • the values you pick, and your note about them
  • your name as you sign it, a picture of your signature and the time you signed
  • your witness's name, a picture of their signature and the time they signed.

Your directive is about your health and your wishes for medical treatment, which the law treats as sensitive. We only collect it because you choose to write it, and you can withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete it.

Your witness

If someone witnesses your directive, we keep their name and signature with your directive. Please tell your witness about this policy.

Technical information

When your device talks to our servers, our hosting providers record technical details, such as IP addresses and times, to keep the service running and secure.

When you contact us

If you email us, we keep your email address and what you send us so we can reply.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your email address, phone number, address, date of birth or Medicare number. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other websites.

3. What stays on your device

Your browser keeps a few things on your device:

  • the code for your link, so this browser can find your directive again
  • your text size setting, and light or dark mode
  • the values you pick, until you finish the questions and they're saved with your directive.

The PDF you download stays on your device. We don't keep a copy of the file.

4. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • save your directive and show it back to you
  • make your PDF
  • let your witness sign
  • keep the website running and secure
  • reply when you contact us.

We don't use your directive for anything else. We don't sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI.

5. Who can see it

Anyone who has your link can see your directive, including your answers and signatures. That's how you share it with your family, your witness and your doctor, without accounts or passwords.

So treat your link like a key. Only share it with people you trust. If someone gets your link who shouldn't have it, email us and we'll delete your directive.

9. Keeping it safe

Your directive is sent over an encrypted connection and stored with a provider that encrypts data. Your link uses a long random code that's very hard to guess.

No system is perfectly safe. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires, where we can reach you.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See it: open your link at any time.
  • Get a copy: download your PDF.
  • Change it: go back to the questions in the same browser, or start a new directive.
  • Delete it: email us your link and ask us to delete it. We'll delete your answers, values and signatures and tell you when it's done.

We can only find your directive with your link, because we don't know who you are.

11. Age

The website is for adults aged 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change matters to you, such as a new service that handles your directive, we'll show a notice on the website before it starts, where we can.

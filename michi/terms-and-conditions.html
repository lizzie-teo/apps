3. What Michi is, and isn't

Michi offers breathing, guided meditations, short lessons, gentle movement, colouring, and places to write down good things and worries. It's there to support your wellbeing from day to day.

Not medical care

Michi isn't therapy, counselling or a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and nothing in it is medical advice. If you're worried about your physical or mental health, talk to a doctor or another health professional. Don't stop or change a treatment because of anything in Michi.

The panic button

The panic button breathes with you while a panic or anxiety attack passes. It doesn't stop or shorten an attack, and it isn't a treatment for panic disorder.

Move with me

Move with me includes walking on the spot, swinging your arms, stretching and star jumps. Only do what feels comfortable. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. If your chest hurts, stop and call 000 or your local emergency number. If you have a heart condition or an injury, or you're pregnant, check with a doctor before you start.

Crisis lines

Other organisations run the crisis lines Michi lists, and their numbers and hours can change. We check them, but we can't promise every line will answer. If one doesn't, call your local emergency number.

5. What you write

It's yours

Your good things, your worries, the steps you plan and the pictures you colour and scribble all belong to you.

To run Michi, we need your permission to store, copy and back up these things, show them back to you, and check them as described below. You give us that permission, for that purpose only. We never sell what you write or use it for advertising.

Automatic checks

Before a good thing or a worry is saved, its words are checked automatically, both on your phone and by a moderation service. A good thing that contains hurtful words won't save until you change them. If anything you write suggests you might hurt yourself, Michi shows you crisis lines first, and your words can still be saved.

Nobody is watching

Nobody reviews what you write, and nobody is told about it. Michi can't send help to you. If you need help now, call a crisis line or emergency services.

Keeping and deleting

  • You can send yourself a copy of everything from the Me tab.
  • You can delete everything from the Me tab. Deleting happens at once and can't be undone.
  • When you let a worry go, it's deleted for good.

  • Sending yourself a copy of everything

Other parts of Michi are free too. Some need the paid plan, and those show a small lock.

The paid plan

  • The paid plan is a subscription. Before you confirm, the app shows its price, how often it renews and any free trial.
  • Apple (the App Store) or Google (Google Play) takes the payment. Your price is in your local currency and includes any tax the store charges.
  • The plan renews automatically at the end of each period, and the store charges you for the next one, until you cancel.

Free trials

When you start a free trial, the store asks for your payment details. When the trial ends, the plan starts and you're charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end. If reminders are on, Michi reminds you two days before the first charge.

Cancelling

Cancel in your phone's subscription settings. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play and go to Payments and subscriptions. Michi can't cancel for you, and deleting the app doesn't cancel the plan. After you cancel, the plan keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle payments, so refunds go through them, under their rules: Apple or Google Play. This doesn't limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If Michi has a problem and the store can't help, contact us.

Price changes

If the price goes up, Apple or Google will tell you before the change, and sometimes ask you to agree to it. You can cancel before the new price starts.
